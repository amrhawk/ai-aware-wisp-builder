# Mini-pilot protocol (3-5 firms)

Purpose: test whether the questionnaire runs, finds real gaps, and is understandable. With so few firms the results do **not** estimate how common any gap is.

## Rules
- Use firms other than the author's employer. Do not use any firm's confidential data, policies, or incident details, even anonymized.
- Each firm answers the questionnaire itself in the browser app and shares **only** the following anonymized numbers (no firm name, no free text).
- Obtain each firm's agreement in writing before the test. The pilot data contain no personal data; testimonials are handled separately (below).
- The author's own time and equipment only; unpaid and non-commercial.

## What each firm reports
1. Size tier (1-5, 6-25, 26+) and whether under 5,000 consumers.
2. Overall score and score per element (copy from the report).
3. Number of full gaps and partial gaps; number of critical gaps.
4. Whether the inventory exercise found at least one AI tool in use that was not on an approved list (yes/no).
5. Minutes taken to complete the questionnaire.
6. Questions they found unclear (IDs only).

## Optional testimonial (separate, written consent)
After the pilot data are collected, you may ask each firm: "Would you be willing to provide a short statement about your experience?" This is optional and independent of the data above.
- Use the consent form in `docs/TESTIMONIAL_CONSENT.md`; obtain written consent before collecting or publishing any statement.
- Keep statements and signed forms **separate from** the anonymized data sheet; do not link a statement to a firm's numbers unless the firm agrees in writing.
- Statements must contain no client data, confidential firm information, or incident details. Offer no payment or benefit.
- Let each firm approve the final wording and choose how it is identified (named, named without firm, or anonymous).

## Analysis
Report: number of firms (N); mean and range of gaps per firm; most common gap elements; share of firms with at least one unapproved AI tool; median completion time; questions revised as a result.

## Results sentence for the paper
"In a preliminary field test with N firms, each identified an average of X gaps (range A to B); the most common were [elements]; Y of N firms had at least one AI tool in use that was not on an approved list; completion took a median of Z minutes."

Fill this in only with real results. Publish anonymized results in version 0.2.
