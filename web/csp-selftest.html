Browser privacy self-test

This page checks that your browser enforces the privacy policy used by the AI-Aware WISP Builder. It tries to load things from a fake outside address (example.invalid, which cannot exist) and reports whether the browser stopped each attempt by policy. Nothing is sent anywhere.
